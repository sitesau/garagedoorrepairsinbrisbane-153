Pre-Repair Checks

If a garage door fails to close or repeatedly reverses, the common cause is often a safety sensor issue. This typically involves the photo-eyes located near the bottom of the tracks becoming misaligned, dirty, or obstructed. Clearing the path for these sensors and observing if the problem persists is a good first step. Persistent faults can also stem from worn rollers, a bent track, or incorrect opener force and travel settings. A broken spring requires a different approach. Springs bear the majority of the door's weight, and a broken one will make the door feel heavy, unbalanced, and unsafe to operate. Forcing the opener in such a situation can lead to bent tracks, strain on the motor, or the door dropping unexpectedly. For issues related to the powered opener, note the remote's behaviour, any motor sounds, where the door stops, and if it reverses.

What to Include in Your Quote Request

A written, fixed-price quote should be provided before any work begins, with figures on the website serving as indicative estimates only. To ensure an accurate quote, provide sufficient detail about the fault rather than a general description of the problem. Key information includes:

  • Door Type: Specify if it is a sectional, roller, or tilt door.
  • Visible Symptom: Describe the issue, such as a broken spring, frayed cable, off-track movement, faulty opener, or damaged panel.
  • Locking Capability: Indicate whether the door can still be locked or secured.
  • Opener Brand: Note the brand of the opener if it is visible.
  • Location: Provide the suburb or postcode.
  • Electrical Work: Mention if electrical work might be involved.

Cost estimates should remain cautious until the door has been assessed. Minor adjustments and simple opener fixes typically cost in the low hundreds of dollars, whereas replacing springs, cables, or motors will incur higher costs. Labour rates and property access can also influence the final price.

Specific Issues: Roller Doors and Openers

A roller door that jams or derails requires specific observations distinct from an opener that hums, strains, or ignores its operational limits. For roller-specific faults, note whether the door curtain or edge is uneven, if rollers or cables appear displaced, and if the door can be moved manually. For opener issues, record the remote response, the motor's sound, and the stopping point. These details serve as inputs for a quote, not a definitive diagnosis. Panel and section replacement is often a solution for storm damage. In such cases, clarify whether the damaged section affects only the appearance or also the door's movement and locking functions. For powered and hardwired opener work, a licensed electrician must perform the task in compliance with the QLD Electrical Safety Act 2002. Mechanical repairs to doors, springs, and tracks do not require an electrician. If a new power point or mains-voltage wiring is needed for the opener, confirm who will undertake this electrical component of the job. For mechanical repairs, ensure the technician will verify the door's balance and normal operation post-repair.

  1. Describe the Symptom. Write down precisely what the garage door does, when it stops, and if it can be locked.
  2. Identify Door Type. Note whether it is a sectional, roller, or tilt door, and record the opener brand if visible.
  3. Check for Obstructions. For closing faults, clear the path of the safety sensors and see if the problem resolves.
  4. Avoid Forcing. If a spring is broken, do not force a heavy or unbalanced door, as this can cause further damage.

Common questions

What are common causes for a garage door not closing? A common cause is misaligned, dirty, or obstructed safety sensors (photo-eyes) near the bottom of the tracks. Other issues can include worn rollers, a bent track, or incorrect opener force and travel settings.

How much do garage door repairs cost in Brisbane? Minor adjustments and opener fixes often cost in the low hundreds of dollars. More significant repairs, such as replacing springs, cables, or motors, will incur higher costs. Labour rates and property access also affect the final price.
